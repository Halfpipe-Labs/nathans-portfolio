0 · DogmaticEmpirical · 100

This score isn't about how intelligent you are — it's about how you relate to ideas you already hold.

At the empirical end, you treat your political views like hypotheses: things to test, update, and revise when evidence changes. At the dogmatic end, your values and identity are more tightly bound to your positions, making them harder to shift.

Neither extreme is purely good or bad. Strong values and consistency matter. But research consistently shows that empirical thinkers are better at updating on new information, finding common ground, and recognising when policies aren't working.
